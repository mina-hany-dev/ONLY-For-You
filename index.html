a letter for you , LOLOLY

There's something I hope you always remember about me.

I know I don't always get it right with you. Sometimes I'm tired, sometimes I'm overwhelmed, and sometimes things come out of me in a way I never meant them to.

And if any of those moments ever made you feel like you weren't loved, appreciated, or important to me... I'm truly sorry. Because nothing could be further from the truth.

You are not a bad sister. You are not someone I don't care about. And you are definitely not someone I could ever just stop loving.

If I ever made you feel otherwise, please know that you were seeing a tired version of me — not the way I see you.

Sometimes I carry too much inside my head, and unfortunately, the people closest to me sometimes see the parts of me that I wish they didn't have to.

But even on the days when I'm quiet, annoyed, distant, or just not myself... your place in my heart doesn't move.

Three years of having you in my life didn't become important because everything was always perfect. It became important because somewhere along the way, you became family. The kind of family I can't imagine my life without.

And I don't want you to remember me only by the moments when I got things wrong. I want you to know that behind all of them, there has always been a brother who loves his sister more than he probably knows how to say.

So please, don't be hard on yourself because of anything I've ever said or done when I wasn't okay.

You are still you. The same girl I'm proud of. The same girl whose laugh can change my whole mood. The same girl who became such an important part of my life without either of us realizing when it happened.

No matter how much things change, no matter how many years pass, and no matter what life brings us...

you will always be my sister. You will always be someone I love. And I will always be grateful that life gave me you.
